How It Sounds is part of numa, a series of minimalist sound journals. Write a short thought, feeling, or memory and hear it interpreted as a twelve-second instrumental.

Each thought and its generated sound become part of a public gallery, with a permanent page to listen, share, or download the audio. No account is required, and submissions are public.
